PERALIYA '26 OFFICIAL RULEBOOK

The official handbook of eligibility, evaluation guidelines, disciplinary codes, and aggregate scoring system for the All-Island Inter-School Media Competition 2026.

1. General Eligibility & Codes of Conduct

The competition is open to all students of schools accredited under the Ministry of Education of Sri Lanka, as well as private and international schools operating within the island.

  • Age Qualification: All contestants must be born on or after February 1, 2007. Proof of age (Birth Certificate or Certified Student Record) must be provided upon request.
  • Official Endorsement: Contestant entries must be verified and endorsed by the Principal or School Coordinator of the respective school's Media Unit.
  • Student Identity: Contestants must present their school identity card or student record book bearing their photograph on the day of on-site competitions.
  • Dress Code: On-site competitors must be attired in their complete, pristine school uniform for all preliminary and final rounds.

2. Judging Panel & Finality of Decisions

The evaluation panel is independent, anonymous prior to competition day, and comprises leading journalists, broadcast anchors, film directors, and university lecturers. The verdict of the judges as ratified by the Principal and Advisory Council of Sirimavo Bandaranaike Vidyalaya shall be final and not subject to appeal.
