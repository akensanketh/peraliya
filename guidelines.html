Institutional Framework

General Guidelines

  • Organizing Body: Peraliya ’26, the All-Island Inter-School Media Day Competition, is organized by the Sisuvisara Media Unit of Sirimavo Bandaranaike Vidyalaya, Colombo 07.
  • Competition Dates: The competition will be conducted online on 10th and 11th October 2026.
  • Age Eligibility: All participants must have been born on or after 31st January 2007 to be eligible to participate in the competition.
  • Nomination Quota: Each participating school may nominate a maximum of two contestants for each competition category. A contestant may participate in only one category.
  • Discipline & Conduct: All participants are expected to maintain proper school discipline and conduct throughout the competition and must comply with the instructions issued by the organizing committee.
  • Official Attire: Participants taking part in News Presenting, Program Presenting, Dubbing, and Sports Commentary must wear their school uniform when recording their submissions.
  • Category WhatsApp Groups: Contestants must join the official WhatsApp group assigned to their respective category, as important instructions, materials, and announcements will be communicated through these groups.
  • Submission Deadlines: All submissions must be made through the official submission method provided by the organizing committee and within the specified deadline. Late submissions will not be accepted.
  • School Code & File Naming: Each participating school will be provided with a unique School Code (e.g. SCH001). Contestants must strictly follow the specified file-naming format when submitting their entries.
  • Self-Introduction: For categories requiring a self-introduction, contestants must clearly provide the required identification details specified under the relevant category guidelines.
  • Panel of Judges: All entries will be assessed by a panel of judges appointed by the organizing committee. The decision of the judging panel shall be final.
  • Disqualification: Any submission that does not comply with the specific requirements of its respective category may be subject to disqualification.
  • Official Channels: Further announcements, instructions, and competition-related updates will be communicated through the official competition communication channels.
Broadcast Stream

News Presenting

  • Languages & Categories: Conducted in English and Sinhala under Junior, Intermediate, and Senior categories.
  • Scripts: Two official scripts will be provided; participants must select one.
  • Segments: Contestants must deliver all segments included in the selected script: a news report, a live report, and a documentary.
  • Self-Introduction: At the beginning of the video, participants must clearly state their name, category, script number, and school name.
  • Video Format: Submissions must be a single, continuous, unedited MP4 video. Any edited or cut video will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Duration: Maximum duration is 4–5 minutes. Exceeding the specified time limit will result in disqualification.
  • Quality: Audio and visual quality must be clear and suitable for evaluation. Contestants must wear their official school uniform.
  • Deadline: Submissions must be made on or before 10th October 2026. Late submissions will not be accepted.
  • Judges' Decision: Judges' decisions are final.
Voice Acting Stream

Dubbing

  • Languages: Conducted in Sinhala and English.
  • Age Categories: Junior (Grades 6 to 9) & Senior (Grades 10 to 11).
  • Material: Scripts and relevant video clips will be shared through the respective WhatsApp groups.
  • Clip Selection: Contestants may select one of the two provided clips for dubbing.
  • Visibility: Both the contestant and the selected video clip must be clearly visible in the submitted video. Must be attired in school uniform.
  • Deadline: Submissions must be completed by midnight on 10th October 2026.
  • Judges' Decision: The judges' decision is final and cannot be challenged.
Media Presenting

Program Presenting

  • Languages & Categories: Conducted in Sinhala and English under Junior, Intermediate, and Senior categories.
  • Script Delivery: Contestants must present all segments provided in the official script.
  • Video Format: Each contestant must submit one single, continuous, unedited MP4 video. Edited or cut videos will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Attire: School uniform is mandatory during recording.
  • Duration: Maximum duration is 4–5 minutes. The specified time limit must not be exceeded.
  • Quality: Audio and visual quality must be clear.
  • Deadline: Submissions must be made by midnight on 10th October 2026. Late submissions will not be accepted.
  • Judges' Decision: Judges' decisions are final.
Live Broadcast

Sports Commentary

  • Category & Language: This is an Open competition conducted in Sinhala and English.
  • Materials: Relevant video clips will be shared through the designated WhatsApp groups.
  • Sport Selection: Participants must select one sport from Cricket, Football, or Rugby and provide commentary based on the assigned video.
  • Visibility: Both the contestant (in school uniform) and selected video clip must be clearly visible.
  • Video Format: The submission must be a single, continuous, unedited video. Editing, cutting, or altering the video will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Deadline: Submissions must be completed before midnight on 10th October 2026.
  • Judges' Decision: The judges' decision is final.
Print Journalism

News Editing

  • Languages & Categories: Conducted in Sinhala and English under Junior, Intermediate, and Senior categories.
  • Timed Window:
    • Junior: 2 hours (10:00 a.m. – 12:00 p.m.)
    • Intermediate and Senior: 3 hours (10:00 a.m. – 1:00 p.m.)
  • Handwritten Requirement: All answers must be handwritten clearly.
  • Format: Completed scripts must be scanned or photographed and submitted as a single PDF.
  • File Naming Format: Strictly SCH001_Name (using your school code and contestant name).
  • Close of Window: Junior submissions close at 12:00 p.m.; Intermediate and Senior submissions close at 1:00 p.m. Late entries will not be accepted.
  • No AI: AI-generated answers are strictly prohibited. Any violation will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Judges' Decision: The judges' decision is final.
Media Technology

Technical

  • Category & Languages: Conducted in Sinhala and English languages under the Open category.
  • Submission Method: Answers must be entered directly into the designated fields in the provided Google Form.
  • Independence & Integrity: Participants must work independently. The use of AI tools is strictly prohibited.
  • Duration: 1 hour 30 minutes.
  • Deadline: Answers must be submitted within the specified time frame. The form will stop accepting responses at the deadline.
  • Penalties: Violation of the rules may result in disqualification.
  • Judges' Decision: The judges' decision is final.
Digital Art

Graphic Designing

  • Category: This is an Open Category competition.
  • Theme & Format: Participants must select one of the themes provided and submit their design as a JPG or PNG file before the announced deadline.
  • Software: Any suitable design software may be used, including Adobe Photoshop and Adobe Illustrator.
  • Originality: The submitted work must be the contestant's original creation.
  • No AI or Plagiarism: Copied content or AI-generated designs will result in immediate disqualification.
Visual Arts

Photography

  • Category & Subcategories: Open Category competition with three subcategories:
    • Colour Photography
    • Monochrome Photography
    • Mobile Photography
  • Submission Limit: Each participant may submit a maximum of three photos per subcategory. Participation in multiple subcategories is permitted.
  • Equipment: Images captured using DSLR or mirrorless cameras are permitted (and mobile cameras for the Mobile Photography subcategory).
  • File Size: Each image file must not exceed 20 MB.
  • RAW Image Verification: If requested by the judging panel, the participant must provide the original RAW image.
  • No Watermarks: Photos must not contain watermarks or the participant's name.
  • Submission Channel: Submissions will be accepted only through the designated submission link and within the announced deadline.
Cinematography

Videography

  • Originality of Footage: All footage must be captured by the contestant.
  • No Pre-existing or AI Footage: Pre-existing footage, AI-generated content, and AI-enhanced visual or audio content are not permitted.
  • Raw Footage Requirement: Contestants must submit both the final video and the original camera footage.
  • Duration: Maximum duration: 2 minutes. Minimum duration: 60 seconds.
  • Watermarks: No watermarks are permitted.
  • Content Standard: Content must be appropriate for a school competition.
  • Permitted Equipment: Any recording device may be used; however, drone cameras are strictly prohibited.
  • Deadline: Submissions must be made within the specified timeline. Late submissions will not be accepted.
Illustration

Cartoon Drawing

  • Category: This is an Open Category competition.
  • Topics: Competition topics will be communicated through the relevant WhatsApp groups.
  • Permitted Mediums: Drawing pencils and black pens may be used. No other colour medium is permitted.
  • Medium & Size: Drawings must be completed on A4-size paper.
  • Submission Method: Participants must upload a photograph of their completed drawing through the designated submission link.
  • Deadline: All drawings must be uploaded before the announced deadline.
