THE LEGACY OF EXCELLENCE

WHAT IS PERALIYA?

"පෙරළිය" (Peraliya) signifies the transformative paradigm shift in modern media — uniting tradition with technological revolution.

In an era where media shapes the way we connect, communicate and create, Peraliya emerges as a platform that brings together young media enthusiasts from across schools. Organized by the Sisuvisara Media Unit of Sirimavo Bandaranaike Vidyalaya, this inter-school media competition aims to celebrate creativity, talent and innovation through a diverse range of media categories.

"Peraliya is not merely a competition, but a space where young creators can showcase their abilities, challenge themselves and inspire one another."

All competition streams are adjudicated by an esteemed, independent panel comprising renowned national television anchors, seasoned photojournalists, cinematic directors, and academic scholars in mass communication to guarantee unwavering integrity and transparent evaluation.
